## Brainstorming before a plan

**When.** The ask is weak (its goal or limits are unclear) or large (several outcomes that could each stand alone). Recommend a brainstorm to the owner in one sentence and let them choose. Never recommend one for a simple ask.

**What it is.** A conversation that settles direction in prose: the outcome wanted, what is out of scope, the options, and the choice. Nothing is stored, and no task drafts are written during it.

**How.** Read what the ask touches first. Offer two or three concrete options with a recommendation, and ask the open questions together, not one at a time. Say what you assumed. Stop when the owner has chosen a direction.

**Then.** Write the task drafts from the settled direction and follow the drafting runbook. A direction that is still open is not ready to be drafted.
