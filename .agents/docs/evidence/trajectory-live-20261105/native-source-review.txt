### Otherwise the delta design checks out

`src/observability/read.ts:2007–2028` binds baselines to project, directory, scope kind/session, and task; the RPC authorizes before reading or touching the cache (`src/observability/rpc.ts:212–225`). Cache state is immutable serialized data, old cursors remain replayable until eviction, and missing/expired/evicted or cross-identity baselines produce a full reset. Cache updates happen only after the existing before/after source checks. Panel patches verify the base cursor and task/project/scope, and task changes, close, and history recapture clear the cursor. Captured history and ordinary paged/HMAC paths remain separate and unchanged.
