## Screenshot findings

1. **P0 — narrow chart is not readable.** The narrow capture compresses the plot vertically and several dollar ticks overlap one another; the axis title and labels consume a large fraction of the plot. The wide capture's ticks are legible. Give the graph enough usable height and choose tick density from available space, rather than removing units or precision indiscriminately.
2. **P0 — graph selection is not understandable.** Both captures show **GPT-6 Luna** selected below, but the plot labels only Kimi K3.1 Pro and Qwen3.8 Flash; the rightmost range is unnamed. The still does not prove which mark represents Luna. Make the selected _plottable_ route visibly identifiable on the chart, including a range or partial mark, and expose friendly model identity plus amount and source in its hover/focus tooltip. Preserve a clear selected state without depending on color. Do not fabricate a point for an unplottable selection.
3. **P1 — accounting dominates the first view.** The captures show “Coverage gaps,” “partial,” source-snapshot counts and a two-line legend including “Priced subtotal · total unknown” and “Priced subtotal range · total unknown.” These compete with model comparison. Use compact consumer-facing mark guidance and put detailed accounting/coverage in tooltip or disclosure, while keeping the fact that a displayed partial amount is not a total immediately understandable. Do not hide actionable stale/error state.
4. **P1 — controls and inspector crowd decision facts.** The narrow filters wrap into two chunky rows; provider and effort also take two large controls, repeating the raw route shown above. The inspector does name GPT-6 Luna and shows `≈$0.352–$0.671 priced portion / accepted task` with `Current market · OpenRouter · current`; that is useful, but the amount, denominator, partial nature and source should read naturally together. Compact controls without sacrificing hit targets or exact route access, and lead with friendly name/effort and a concise truthful cost summary. Raw provider/model IDs and deep basis belong in accessible secondary detail. The screenshot does not establish the availability of quality, sample, time or token facts; show only supplied facts.
